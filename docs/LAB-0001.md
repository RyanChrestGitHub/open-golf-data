# Laboratory 0001 — Synthetic baseline and first physical evidence protocol

**Status:** Synthetic baseline runnable. Physical validation pending.

## Hypotheses

- E001 Access: A permitted, supported interface exposes individual source observations without interfering with normal simulator function.
- E002 Attribution: At least one trusted identity signal can associate observations with a consenting golfer; otherwise they remain unclaimed.
- E003 Fidelity: Every accessible exposed record can be persisted and exported without losing native fields, silently changing values or inventing missing ones.

## Synthetic lab

Replay `open_golf_data/fixtures/synthetic_session.json`. It contains fictional ball and club data from a pseudo-device and simulated carry from a pseudo-simulator, plus two fictional players, two installation modes and an event without attribution.

Independent tests assert: idempotent replays, immutable original bytes, distinct measurement kinds, preservation of future fields, failure on source-ID conflicts, cross-installation history and exclusion of unclaimed observations.

**Important:** Five replayed fixture observations are *not* five independently verified physical swings.

## First real-source protocol (subject to venue authorization)

1. Record venue permission, software versions, launch-monitor model and fixture handling agreement. Do not modify or intercept live connections without permission.
2. Select a short controlled protocol: approximately 20–30 deliberately counted attempts, including alternating clubs, at least two intentionally different participants when the operator permits, rejected or repeated attempts, and a software restart if safe.
3. Record independent physical attempt times and counts; acquire authorized source-native files/exports, then compare to the collector. Never assume native export is independent physical ground truth.
4. Maintain a table connecting (or declining to connect) physical attempts, device detections, exposed records and captured records. Preserve unresolved gaps explicitly.
5. Inventory each field, available units, null behavior and whether the manufacturer documents it as measured, estimated or simulated. Do not reclassify solely based on a column's name.
6. Publish only a privacy-reviewed, sanitized fixture. Raw live recordings remain private until the contributor or venue explicitly approves distribution.
7. Produce an evidence-based report: access path, observed coverage denominator, field fidelity, ambiguity, failure modes, and software/version-specific status.

## Four denominators

- `physical_attempts`: independently counted during a controlled test.
- `device_detected`: established only if device detection evidence exists.
- `source_exposed`: established only if source records can be independently enumerated.
- `collector_captured`: locally stored source observations.

Do not report a percentage unless the denominator can be established and each event can be reconciled sufficiently to compare sets. We can prove equality to an export without proving it is the complete set of device detections.

## Promotion rule

Physical compatibility is not verified until an authorized device operator signs off a repeatable session and version-specific evidence supports the claim. A parser passing fixtures is `replay-validated`, not `hardware-validated`.
